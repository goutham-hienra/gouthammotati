A — Brushed gunmetal

repeating-linear-gradient(90deg, .018 white, 1px stripes)
+ cool inset top highlight rgba(190,210,230,.10)
+ SVG fractal-noise overlay at mix-blend-mode: overlay
+ four-edge inset shadows (top/bottom/left/right) for chiselled feel

B — Polished slate

radial-gradient(... at 50% -25%, .045 warm)
+ warm inset top sheen rgba(255,235,220,.08)
+ narrow horizontal highlight line across the top edge
+ no grain, no noise — smooth dielectric surface

Both keep the hairline border 1px solid var(--border-ghost) and no outer drop-shadow, so the “carved” direction is preserved.
