# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Directors and VPs of Design evaluating a senior IC or lead designer for enterprise/B2B roles. 3-minute decision window: they are skimming for signal, not reading a resume. Secondary: recruiters and hiring teams at target companies.

Context: they're senior enough to read craft quality directly from the work. They distrust self-description and cliché. They skip portfolios that lead with methodology. They slow down for specificity: a real number, a concrete constraint, a design decision defended.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Goutham Motati, Lead Product Designer with 10 years across enterprise software, conversion design, design systems, and 0→1 product work. Goal: secure senior IC or lead design roles (full-time or consulting), remote/hybrid, Indian and international employers.

Success: a Director opens the portfolio cold, sees work they can evaluate in 90 seconds, and sends a message within the same session.

## Brand Personality

Quiet editorial authority. The work speaks; the chrome frames without competing.

Three words: precise, confident, unhurried.

Tone: "Elegant bold with character" — selective scale and weight contrast at key moments (name block, case study titles, section anchors); disciplined restraint everywhere else. Bold is earned, not spread. Character comes from committed decisions, not decorative noise.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS portfolio: identical card grid, hero-metric template (big number, small label, gradient accent), every section wearing the same layout
- LinkedIn-style timeline dumps: chronological, role-focused, no design thinking visible
- Awwwards-chasing: scroll-triggered reveals, glassmorphism as decoration, animation as substitute for content
- Italic-everything "character": applying expressive type (Fraunces italic) across repeated UI elements like metadata rows — it reads as loud, not bold. Italic Fraunces is reserved for singular moments only.
- Green CTAs adjacent to green availability indicators: semantic collision. Green = state, not action.
- Orange as brand accent: photo-orange (#f0622e) is sampled from the profile photo backdrop and scoped to the portrait glow only. Not a brand color.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Case studies lead with outcomes and decisions. Process methodology is Phase 3 material, never Phase 1.
2. **Restraint earns the bold moments.** Champagne and maroon are finite. Every usage has a job. Neither is used for decoration.
3. **The hero-metric template is banned.** Outcomes live in the work, not as big-number stat grids floating in a hero section.
4. **Editorial register, not SaaS landing page.** The portfolio is a curated publication. Sections have editorial voice. Copy earns every word.
5. **The work is the product; the chrome is the frame.** Typography, spacing, and color exist to surface the case studies, not to compete with them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (already implemented with `animation:none` / `transition:none` override). Color contrast checked against obsidian canvas. No information conveyed by color alone.
