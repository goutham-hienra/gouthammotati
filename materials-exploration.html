Technical Notes

Current (brushed gunmetal): repeating-linear-gradient(90deg) horizontal stripes + SVG fractal-noise at mix-blend-mode:overlay. Gives the sharpest tactile grain. Cool top-edge light for depth.

Brushed aluminum: Vertical grain (180deg) instead of horizontal. Octaves and frequency tuned down for finer texture. Reads lighter and more precision-tooled.

Leather/suede: Dense four-octave fractal with mix-blend-mode:multiply (darkens grain rather than overlays). Warm diagonal gradient. Feels organic and aged.

Polished stone: No grain texture — instead, a soft top-edge hairline sheen with a clean radial gradient. Mimics light reflection off a smooth, curved surface. Most minimal.

Frosted glass: Uses backdrop-filter:blur(12px) on the card background itself, plus a very fine high-frequency fractal noise. Reads contemporary and translucent. May need perf testing in browsers with weak GPU support.

Woven canvas: Dual diagonal repeating-linear-gradients at 45° and -45° to create a cross-hatch weave. Organic, textile-like feel. No fractal grain — pure geometric pattern.

Flat minimal: Zero texture. Hairline border with inset glow: champagne top edge at .18 alpha (soft light from above), dark bottom edge at .30 alpha (shadow). Radial gradient overlay in champagne at .14 alpha, plus minimal vertical gradient for depth. The border itself appears to catch light. Inspired by healthcare dashboards (Apple Health, Fitbit). Uses your obsidian palette with brand-color accents.
